Our Story

Rooted in Srinagar, Serving All India

Abalfazl Travels is a trusted Kashmir travel agency based at M.K Chowk, Hawal, Srinagar — in the heart of the valley we love. Founded with a passion for sharing Kashmir's breathtaking beauty with the world, we have been crafting memorable journeys for travellers from across India and beyond.

Our team of local Kashmiri experts knows every hidden meadow, every winding mountain road, and every authentic chai spot. From the serene Dal Lake to the adventure trails of Gurez Valley, we ensure every trip is personal, safe, and unforgettable.

We offer complete travel services — tour packages, houseboat and hotel bookings, cab rentals, air and train tickets, Gulmarg Gondola tickets, and B2B travel solutions. Our promise is simple: transparent pricing, genuine hospitality, and your complete satisfaction.
